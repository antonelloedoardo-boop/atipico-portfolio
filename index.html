I’m (24), co-owner and art director of Atipico, an underground electronic music event format based in Northern Italy. Since 2024, I have led the development of Atipico’s brand, from strategy to creative execution.

Alongside logistics and operations, I lead the brand’s art direction: I define the musical direction, manage artist bookings and work closely with our graphic designer to translate concepts into visuals. I draw on art and nightlife culture, balancing market insight with a clear identity, without ever compromising it.

The project

Three non-negotiables, and the best we can squeeze out of everything around them.

01 · Event production

Every night is produced to a high standard, from sound to space, whatever the budget.

02 · Music

Line-ups curated for quality and coherence, night after night.

03 · Communication

Every touchpoint, from posters and posts to merch, is held to the same standard as the night itself.

Around these, we push for the best in everything we touch. That’s how Atipico grows on a lean budget, without ever selling out.

Atipico’s manifesto

Brand identity

Hand-drawn lettering, a star and a single ink. The visual language fuses 90s London rave flyers with screen printing, and turns what’s raw, underground and authentic into its biggest asset.

90s London rave flyers

Stacked, centred info, clashing typefaces, stars and “featuring” lines: the DIY energy of photocopied flyers.

Screen printing

One ink, printed solid, every time. Each piece is designed to be pulled by hand in a single colour.

Raw and authentic

Hand-drawn lines and visible flaws, nothing polished away. Being underground is our point of difference, not something to hide.

Instagram flyers

Animated flyers for past Atipico nights, made for Instagram, then printed and put up around town: one design, online and offline.

Merch

Every piece starts from one of our own event posters and is screen printed by hand in small runs. It drops at the merch stand on the night first, then online.

From poster to merch

Campaigns

Four campaigns: three prints lifted from our posters, and a gift keychain cut from the logo.

Dancing figures

White tee, one red ink: dancing figures, stars and a moon orbiting the logo. Shot against a blue wall, on a red bench and in front of a pink curtain.

Crying star boy

One graphic, two colourways: acid green on an olive tee, white on a royal blue crewneck. Shot on a washing line against a red sky.

Silly Billy

Black long-sleeve, one pink ink: star-eyed heads and the line “You’re alive, act like it”. Shot in rooms full of music references.

Atipico keychain

A gift for our crowd: a metal keychain cut in the shape of the logo. Launched with an X-ray scan of a tote bag, status: “Ready for the dancefloor”.
